[WIP] Just some books I thought were interesting or helpful. Something like a small reading list There is some small bit on aviation CRM, checklists, human factors, and incident-minded thinking, since that mindset maps surprisingly well onto homelab operations and systems work.

  • The C Programming Language – Kernighan & Ritchie
  • Introduction to Algorithms – Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein (CLRS)
  • Operating Systems Three Easy Pieces – Remi Arpaci-Dusseau & Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau
  • UNIX Systems Programming – Kay A. Robbins & Steven Robbins
  • Digraphs: Theory, Algorithms and Applications – Jørgen Bang-Jensen & Gregory Z. Gutin
  • The Little Book of Semaphores – Allen Downey
  • Meditations – Marcus Aurelius
  • Trauma Team Dynamics – Lawrence Marshall Gillman & Sandy Widder
  • Crew Resource Management Training (120-51B/C/D/E) – Federal Aviation Administration
  • Risk Management Handbook (FAA-H-8083-2A) – Federal Aviation Administration
  • Human Factors Handbook (NASA-HDNK-8709.25) – National Aeronautics and Space Administration
  • Crew Resource Management for Automated Teammates (CRM-A) – Robert J. Shively, Joel Lachter, Robert Koteskey and Summer L. Brandt
