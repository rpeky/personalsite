HP Z640 Workstation/Server

Current proof of concept for my homelab, running proxmox as a hypervisor to run vms that make the core of my network, automation and services

Uses a mix of zfs stripped mirroring of the NVME drives on a x4 biurcation card in the machine, regular 2.5"" SSDs, and NAS storage
