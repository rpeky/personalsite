Managed Switch

Backbone layer for internal traffic routing, link aggregation, and VLAN segmentation.

Hardware

  • Model: Mikrotik CRS310-8G+2S+IN
  • 8x 2.5 Gigabit Ethernet port
  • 2x SFP+ ports
  • Marvell 98DX226S switch-chip, dual-core ARM CPU, 256MB RAM

Functions

  • Point-to-point traffic from NAS, servers, desktops, edge compute
  • VLAN tagging, connected through RJ45
